   Three slopes worth naming:

      steep   RTF > 1   faster than reality  (what a twin wants)
      45 deg  RTF = 1   keeping pace         (a live mirror)
      shallow RTF < 1   slower than reality  (fine for study,
                                              fatal for forecast)
